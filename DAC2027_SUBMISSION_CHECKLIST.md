# DAC 2027 Research Manuscript Submission Checklist

## Official deadlines

- Abstract and complete author list: November 11, 2026 at 5 PM US Pacific Time.
- Manuscript: November 18, 2026 at 5 PM US Pacific Time.

## Manuscript format

- PDF only.
- Letter page size.
- Double column.
- 9 or 10 point body font.
- Up to six body pages and one additional references only page.
- IEEE conference format.
- Approximately 100 word abstract that states contribution, impact, and results.
- Leave room for author names and acknowledgements in the accepted version.

## Double blind checks

- No author names, affiliations, email addresses, acknowledgements, lab names, repository owner names, or identifying URLs in the manuscript or abstract.
- Cite prior work by any author in the third person.
- Do not use “omitted for blind review.”
- Clear PDF author, creator, producer, subject, and identifying custom metadata.
- Confirm figures, screenshots, paths, filenames, and artifact links do not reveal identity.

## Evidence checks

- P1 two scenario post freeze COMSOL gate passes.
- P2 four method development comparison and ablations pass.
- P2 is labelled project authored development evidence.
- P3 has 24 externally authored and separately adjudicated tasks from eight new families.
- P3 contains all 192 frozen observations.
- P3 main result table is generated without post unseal method changes.
- Every number in the abstract, results, tables, and conclusion maps to a frozen JSON or CSV artifact.
- No template, synthetic, partial, or internally authored record is presented as independent evidence.

## AI content statement

- Keep the section heading `Content Generated by AI`.
- Identify the systems used and the sections or artifacts they helped draft.
- State that human authors verified claims, citations, calculations, and final wording.
- Do not list an AI system as an author.

## Submission system checks

- Enter every author and affiliation before the abstract deadline.
- Complete all conflicts of interest.
- Complete the duplicate submission disclosure.
- Select AI1.5 as the primary category and AI1.1 as the secondary category.
- Select up to two topic areas and up to three keywords.
- Upload the final anonymous PDF and open it from the submission system.
- Obtain explicit approval from every human author before the manuscript deadline.
- Generate `human_author_approval.template.json` only after the sealed P3 report passes all count and hash checks.
- Require all six human checks and exact manuscript/P3 SHA-256 matches before accepting `human_author_approval.json`.
